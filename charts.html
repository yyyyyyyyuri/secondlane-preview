Stat tiles

One number per tile, with its change and a sparkline of the last twelve months. The change is an arrow and words, never red or green: the palette has no status colours.

    06 · Rules

    How the charts behave

    • Order is fixed. Series take clay, terracotta, amber, bronze in that order and keep their colour when filters change. A fifth and sixth take ochre and olive and must be labelled directly; past that, group into Other or split into small charts.
    • SL Yellow means SecondLane. Only SecondLane’s own deals, prices or the reader’s position. On linen, SecondLane is bottle green, since yellow on linen can’t be read. Never use yellow with ochre in the same chart.
    • Other is sand. Moss stays out of data (too close to olive).
    • Text is ink, never a series colour. Values, labels and legends use the text colours; the swatch next to them carries the identity.
    • Labels where it’s low contrast. Terracotta and bronze are dim on green; clay, amber and ochre are pale on linen. Those marks always carry a direct label or sit beside a table.
    • One axis. No dual-axis charts. Index both to 100 or draw two charts.
    • Marks. Lines 2px, dots 8px with a 2px ground ring, bars rounded 4px at the data end only, 2px gaps between fills. Grid lines at 10% ink, horizontal only.
    • Hover. Lines get a crosshair and a tooltip with every series at that point; bars, segments and slices get their own tooltip. The tooltip is the inverse ground.
    • Finding as headline. Each chart’s title says what it shows (“AI’s share fell every quarter”), the eyebrow names the measure, the footer names the source.
    • Accessible. Every chart has a text alternative, a table view on the platform, and works without hover. Motion is off under reduced motion.
