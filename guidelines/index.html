Overview · Principles

A private office, not a trading app

SecondLane should feel calm, established, discreet and expensive. When a decision is not covered by these guidelines, these four rules settle it.

Quiet by default

Bottle green and linen carry the page. Yellow is a signal, used once or twice a screen, never as decoration.

Editorial type

A serif for headlines, a clean sans for everything else. Large, well spaced, sentence case.

Real objects

Warm editorial photography and halftone objects from the sectors we cover. No stock clichés, no neon.

Careful words

Pre-licence wording everywhere: opportunities and introductions, never trades. See Standards.
