01 · Principles

A private office, not a trading app

SecondLane should feel calm, established, discreet and expensive. When a decision is not covered by these guidelines, these four rules settle it.

Quiet by default

Bottle green and linen carry the page. Yellow is a signal, used once or twice a screen, never as decoration.

Editorial type

A serif for headlines, a clean sans for everything else. Large, well spaced, sentence case.

Real objects

Warm editorial photography and halftone objects from the sectors we cover. No stock clichés, no neon.

Careful words

Pre-licence wording everywhere: opportunities and introductions, never trades. See section 10.

02 · Logo

Logo

The logo is the diamond mark with the SecondLane wordmark, supplied as one SVG (assets/logo.svg, 128 × 32 viewBox). Use it as a single colour: linen on green, green on linen or white.

Clear space

Keep at least half the logo height free on every side (16px at the 32px nav size).

Sizes

Nav: 128 × 32px. Footer: 112 × 28px. Minimum: 96px wide on screen. Never set it smaller than the body text.

Implementation

Inline the SVG or use it as a CSS mask so the colour follows a token. Give the link aria-label="SecondLane home".

Don't

07 · Imagery

Imagery

Two approved styles: warm editorial photography for people and places, and halftone objects for the sectors SecondLane covers. Plus one generated pattern, the dot field.

Photography

Do
  • Natural light, warm tones, real materials: stone, wood, wool, brass.
  • Quiet, unposed moments; people seen at work, not smiling at the camera.
  • Leave calm space for text when the image sits behind type.
Don't
  • Filters, heavy grading, neon or yellow light.
  • Trading screens, charts on monitors, handshakes, piles of cash.
  • Stock photography that looks staged.

Halftone objects

  • One object per image from a SecondLane sector: AI, crypto, fintech, consumer tech, gaming, space, deep tech, autonomy, defense, energy.
  • Dark version: object on #12301F with a soft dot halo that fades out, never an opaque glow. Light version: object alone on #F5F3EF.
  • One small terracotta or bronze accent per image. No yellow or neon inside images: yellow belongs to the interface.

08 · Motion

Motion

Slow and soft: things settle rather than snap. Motion only responds to the visitor or reveals content once. Hover a row to play it.

Card lift · 6px up, .7s, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)
Reveal · 24px rise plus fade, .9s, items 160ms apart, once per page view
Controls · buttons and links .18s; press 1px down in .08s
Edges and nav · lit edge and nav background .35s ease
Panels · tabs and accordions, --ease: cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)
Do
  • Animate transform and opacity only.
  • Honour prefers-reduced-motion: no lifts, rises or moving dots; everything visible at once.
  • Keep content readable if scripts fail: hidden-until-revealed content must default to visible.
Don't
  • Parallax, auto-playing carousels, tilt or bouncing easings.
  • Zoom images on hover.
  • Loop anything for more than 5 seconds without a pause control.
09 · Accessibility

Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page. Most of it comes for free from the palette and the components above; these are the parts to check in the build.

Contrast

Body text at least 4.5:1, large text and UI 3:1. Every token pair in section 3 passes; yellow never sits on light grounds as text.

Keyboard

Everything reachable by Tab in reading order. Visible focus: 2px outline in the text colour, 3px offset. A skip link to the main content.

Targets

At least 44 × 44px for anything clickable, including the arrow links and accordion rows.

Structure

One H1 per page, headings in order, landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), tabs with proper ARIA roles.

Forms

Real labels, errors in text, autocomplete set (email), confirmation announced with role="status".

Motion and media

Reduced motion respected; decorative canvases aria-hidden; meaningful images described in alt.

10 · Words and compliance

Words and compliance

SecondLane is an introduction network until its licence is live. Interface copy, button labels, alt text and metadata all follow the pre-licence glossary. Final copy comes from the client's copy deck; Omar signs it off.

Required disclaimer

SecondLane is an introduction network and is not a registered broker-dealer in the United States.

Show it under the home hero and in every footer. Pages with opportunities also carry a risk line, for example: "Private company interests are illiquid and you may lose the full amount you put in. Nothing on this page is an offer to buy or sell, or a recommendation."

FINRA Rule 2210: scarcity and timing are fine ("closes when the company lists"); promised or implied returns, "guaranteed", price predictions and fake countdowns are not. Figures in square brackets in the prototype, such as $[X]B, are placeholders for real data.
