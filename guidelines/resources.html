0.211 Nov 2026Handoff. New page Patterns: the hero with its halftone edge, section head, feature columns and steps, accordion with an image strip, image band, sign-up card, call-to-action strip, closing band, dark panel, FAQ, motion and imagery rules, as built on the live pages. 0.18 Nov 2026Colours aligned to the palette agreed in Round 3 (Option C, Warm earth). Shade ramps, Green 2, Stone, Mute, Rust, On yellow and White removed; sand and moss are the support colours; clay, terracotta, amber, bronze, ochre and olive are the chart series. Hover states use a glow ring instead of extra shades. Sections are green or linen. Tokens, Tailwind theme, DESIGN.md and React starters regenerated. Photography rules rewritten for the Round 6 style: people together in elevated spaces, no detail or furniture shots; four new example photos. Visual basics PDF (3 pages: logo and type, colour, visuals) added for sharing.
0.17 Nov 2026First release for the platform team. Guide split into Overview, Foundations, Components, Standards, Tokens and code, Resources. Colour ramps with role tokens and light and dark proportions. 27 component specs with anatomy, states, phone and accessibility notes. React starter components. DESIGN.md, Tailwind v4, CSS variables and W3C design tokens.

Coming next: page templates and the CMS content model, content and data rules, engineering standards (icons, performance, browser support, testing), and governance.
