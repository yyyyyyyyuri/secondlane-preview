Standards

Standards · Accessibility

Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page. Most of it comes for free from the palette and the components above; these are the parts to check in the build.

Contrast

Body text at least 4.5:1, large text and UI 3:1. Every token pair on the Foundations page passes; yellow never sits on light grounds as text.

Keyboard

Everything reachable by Tab in reading order. Visible focus: 2px outline in the text colour, 3px offset. A skip link to the main content.

Targets

At least 44 × 44px for anything clickable, including the arrow links and accordion rows.

Structure

One H1 per page, headings in order, landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), tabs with proper ARIA roles.

Forms

Real labels, errors in text, autocomplete set (email), confirmation announced with role="status".

Motion and media

Reduced motion respected; decorative canvases aria-hidden; meaningful images described in alt.

Words and compliance

Required disclaimer

SecondLane is an introduction network and is not a registered broker-dealer in the United States.

Show it under the home hero and in every footer. Pages with opportunities also carry a risk line, for example: "Private company interests are illiquid and you may lose the full amount you put in. Nothing on this page is an offer to buy or sell, or a recommendation."

FINRA Rule 2210: scarcity and timing are fine ("closes when the company lists"); promised or implied returns, "guaranteed", price predictions and fake countdowns are not. Figures in square brackets in the prototype, such as $[X]B, are placeholders for real data.
