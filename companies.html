What is the secondary market for private shares?

The terms used across SecondLane, defined in plain language.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ
A

Accredited investor

A person or entity that meets the US income or net worth thresholds set by the SEC to take part in private offerings.

D

Dedicated vehicle

A separate entity set up to hold one opportunity, so each participant's money goes only to that opportunity.

I

Introduction

SecondLane connecting an existing holder with an interested party. Each side agrees terms directly, under documented checks.

L

Lock-up

A period after a listing when early holders cannot sell their shares, usually 90 to 180 days.

Q

Qualified participant

Someone outside the US who meets the eligibility rules of their own jurisdiction to take part in private market opportunities.

R

Right of first refusal

The company's right to buy shares on the same terms before a holder can transfer them to someone else.

S

Secondary market

Where existing holders of private company interests meet interested parties, before the company lists.

T

Tender offer

A company-run programme that lets employees and early holders turn ownership interest into cash before a listing.

Transfer approval

The company's sign-off on a change of holder, set out in its own documents.
